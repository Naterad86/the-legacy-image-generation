The experiment asks:

> Can the canonical Legacy emblem be embodied once, completely, and natively in photoreal exterior stone, photoreal interior stone, and painterly cloud material—without reframing, motif multiplication, rendering-language mismatch, or unrelated redesign?

Anything below `EXCELLENT` is a failure. Every failure must still produce generalized causal reasoning and a path to stabilization.

## Classification

- `FAIL — ONE CORRECTION ELIGIBLE` — below excellence because of exactly one bounded defect correctable by one fresh generation that preserves every frozen source, surface, mechanism, and held constant.
- `FINAL FAIL` — multiple independent defects; required redesign; unbounded correction; or any result below excellence after the permitted `C1`.

Every failure report must include:

1. direct visual observations;
