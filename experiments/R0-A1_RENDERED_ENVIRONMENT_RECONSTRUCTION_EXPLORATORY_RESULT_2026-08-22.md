## Generalized finding

It understood the subject as “Oslo Opera House with sloped marble roof, glass volume, plaza, and cranes,” but treated the source as a place reference to reconstruct rather than as fixed image-space geometry to re-render.

The least standardized mechanism is therefore refined to:

> **source-image geometric lock during rendering-modality transfer**
