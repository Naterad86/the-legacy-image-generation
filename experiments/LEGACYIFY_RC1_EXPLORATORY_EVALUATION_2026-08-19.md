## Observations by candidate

### Candidate 1

- The base coastline, road, skyline, and foreground activity remained recognizable.
- A large perspective-aware architectural use of the emblem demonstrated a promising scene-native mechanism.
- The transformation over-applied black-and-gold treatment and repeated approximate motifs across multiple surfaces.
- Repetition and palette dominance made the result feel imposed rather than governed with restraint.

### Candidate 2

- Restraint and base-scene preservation improved.
- The large architectural emblem retained the promising perspective shift.
- The emblem at approximately `(x: 99.3%, y: 23.0%)` was truncated by the right image boundary. Nate classified that imperfection as intolerable.
- Surfboard applications remained approximations rather than perfect canonical embodiments.
- Any sky modification near approximately `(x: 24.4%, y: 16.2%)` was imperceptible or absent. Nate suggested that a cumulonimbus surface might support a future environmental embodiment, as an example rather than an authorization for this cycle.

### Candidate 3

- The architectural emblem was moved fully in-frame and given deliberate clearance; its perspective-aware façade treatment was substantially stronger.
- The original beach scene remained unmistakable.
- Two surfboard marks remained approximate and therefore below the standard required for literal stamping.
- A large cloud-outline emblem was introduced. It demonstrated an imaginative environmental direction, but it was not a volumetric cumulonimbus embodiment and was outside the bounds of the attempted correction.
- The cloud also changed the visual hierarchy and confounded assessment of the architectural repair.
- Nate's curved assessment: much better and, despite the out-of-bounds cloud addition, “pretty remarkable.”

## Durable findings

1. **Shape before palette.** The emblem's actual visual topology is the primary carrier of Legacy identity. Black-and-gold treatment is secondary and cannot substitute for the shape.
2. **Perspective-aware embodiment is promising.** Reimagining the emblem through a scene-native architectural surface is the strongest useful mechanism demonstrated in this cycle, even though the geometry remains crude.
3. **Literal stamping is allowed only at canonical quality.** A stamp may be useful, but every occurrence must be a precise, deliberate embodiment rather than a hurried approximation.
4. **Restraint is mandatory.** Permission to use the motif does not imply permission to repeat it across every available surface.
5. **Full containment is non-negotiable.** Truncation is an automatic critical defect, irrespective of the quality of the remaining image.
6. **Cloud embodiment remains untested.** The Candidate 3 cloud is exploratory evidence only. It cannot establish a cumulonimbus mechanism or be folded into the next architectural test.

## Evaluation conclusion

**Potential ceiling demonstrated; controlled repeatability not established.**

Candidate 3 is retained because it reveals a valuable design direction. It is not a PASS, a baseline, a canonical source, or proof that the mechanism can repeat reliably. No candidate from this cycle may be used as the starting image or motif authority for a controlled successor cycle.

## Hard stop

The exploratory RC1 cycle ends after Candidate 3. Do not generate a Candidate 4, repair Candidate 3, crop it, remove its cloud, perfect its surfboards, or select a best-of descendant inside this cycle.

## Recommended next cycle — not frozen

### `L0-E` — Perspective-Aware Environmental Embodiment

**Recommendation state:** PROPOSED; not accepted, frozen, preregistered, or authorized to run.

**Question:** Can one complete Legacy emblem-shape embodiment be integrated into a designated architectural surface, under perspective, while preserving the original unbranded environment and avoiding motif multiplication?

**Single mechanism:** One scene-native architectural relief or façade integration derived directly from `LEGACY_LOGO_MASTER`.

**Cycle plan:** Three independent generations using the identical frozen prompt, each starting from the exact original `BASE_SCENE_INPUT`, never from Candidates 1–3.

**Held constants:**

- original 4:3 framing, camera, coastline, road, skyline, buildings, vehicles, people, foreground surfboards, golf bag, and scene identity;
- one predesignated, fully in-frame façade envelope with fixed safe margins;
- identical emblem scale, orientation, viewing perspective, and material instruction;
- the base coastal palette outside the target surface;
- no crop and no target contact with any canvas edge.

**Exclusions:**

- no sky or cloud alteration;
- no surfboard, sign, vehicle, railing, or secondary stamps;
- no additional emblem echoes or generic gold arches;
- no generalized black-and-gold wash;
- no typography or extra branding;
- no new objects or broad scene redesign.

**Separate Evaluation gates:**

- complete, untruncated visibility with deliberate clearance;
- recognizability from emblem shape rather than color;
- preservation of mirrored topology, defining silhouettes, central rise, and major negative spaces;
- coherent perspective, material, lighting, and architectural integration;
- exactly one authorized motif;
- no material change outside the target envelope;
- unmistakable preservation of the original beach environment.

**Repeatability rule:** Three of three conforming outcomes support the mechanism; two of three provide partial support; zero or one does not support it. Stop after the three scheduled outputs. Do not repair, crop, add a cloud follow-up, or generate replacements inside the cycle.

If `L0-E` earns support, recommend a separate `L0-S` Selective Perfect Stamping cycle for one designated surface. Treat a cloud or cumulonimbus embodiment as a later, independent surface-material test.
