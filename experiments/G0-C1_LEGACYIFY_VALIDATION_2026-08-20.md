## Round 1 result

**Classification:** `FINAL FAIL` under the excellence-only trial rule.

The concept succeeded: one floral/wildflower embodiment read immediately as the Legacy motif and occupied a coherent meadow plane without becoming a literal gold stamp.

The execution failed for multiple independent reasons:

1. the output changed the source's framing and added foreground because the ratio and no-expansion constraint had not been stated explicitly;
2. the emblem used fine, bead-like floral particles instead of the source's larger pixel clusters;
3. several topology relationships were simplified below the excellence threshold.

The generalized cause was control relaxation: the model created room and geometric resolution for the motif by quietly relaxing the frame and rendering-language constants. The output was not edited or used as successor authority.

## Single correction design

`G0-C1` restarted from the untouched `BASE_SCENE_INPUT` and exact `LEGACY_LOGO_MASTER`. It did not edit or reference the failed descendant.

The correction made the previously relaxed constants explicit:

- preserve the same full 16:9 composition and perceptual framing;
- no crop, expansion, outpainting, or added foreground;
- modify only one contiguous portion of the existing right foreground meadow;
- use wildflower and grass density to form exactly one complete emblem;
- fit the motif inside the meadow with deliberate margin, shrinking or placing it higher when needed;
- preserve the opposing lion profiles, central interlace, principal curves, and negative spaces;
- use the same chunky pixel clusters, edge stair-stepping, stem widths, and detail frequency already present in the source;
- exclude the wordmark, dividers, gold wash, extra motifs, and unrelated scene changes;
- return one output only.
