## Frozen design target candidate

Create a high-end cinematic 3D CGI successor of the exact scene. Preserve the recognizable Oslo composition and geometry while redesigning materials and lighting into a disciplined Legacy environment: deep charcoal structural accents, controlled blackened metal and glass, warm metallic-gold or golden-orange illumination, cool deep shadows, physically credible stone, and polished commercial finish.

Form exactly one complete Legacy emblem from the plaza's own stone-joint and neighboring-value organization, correctly projected on the ground plane with generous containment. The actual logo topology is primary; gold coloration is optional and cannot compensate for shape drift. Do not add the wordmark, signs, apparel marks, secondary emblems, or logo-shaped reflections.

The result must look intentionally rendered, not like a photograph with synthetic decoration pasted onto it.

## Excellence gates

1. **Canonical topology:** unmistakable canonical emblem shape and major negative spaces.
2. **Exactly one embodiment:** no second stamp, reflection, signage, wordmark, or motif echo.
3. **Complete containment:** no truncation, obstruction, edge collision, or cramped placement.
4. **Scene continuity:** base subject, camera, composition, major geometry, and objects remain recognizable and unmoved.
5. **Rendered intent:** consistently authored high-end CGI; no raw-photo/synthetic middle state.
6. **Modality-native craft:** materials, light, perspective, scale, and detail frequency are coherent.
7. **Durable grammar:** premium hierarchy, warm/cool discipline, restrained gold accent, confident readability, and no blanket black-and-gold wash.
8. **No unauthorized redesign:** only released properties change; no characters, new architecture, typography, or unrelated additions.
9. **Release-level finish:** no crude topology, seams, malformed people or structures, hasty marks, or visible compositing artifacts.

Anything below `EXCELLENT` is failure. A failure remains useful only when it states the generalized cause and path to stabilize.
