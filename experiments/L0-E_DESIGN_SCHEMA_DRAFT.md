## Accepted mechanism boundary

- Introduce exactly one scene-native architectural relief or façade integration derived directly from the canonical emblem.
- Shape recognition is primary; black-and-gold color is secondary.
- Permit only the deformation required for projective perspective, architectural material, depth, and scene-native integration.
- Preserve recognizable mirrored topology, defining lion silhouettes, central rise, and major negative spaces.
- Preserve the base scene outside the predesignated target envelope.
- Do not test literal stamping, atmospheric embodiment, or broad Legacy styling in this cycle.

No model-generated score automatically advances the program.

## Working held constants

- exact original base-scene bytes for every cycle;
- original 4:3 framing and perceptual camera position;
- coastline, road, skyline, buildings, terrain, vehicles, people, foreground surfboards, golf bag, and scene identity;
- one predesignated target surface and normalized envelope;
- one emblem, one scale band, one orientation, one perspective relationship, and one material treatment;
- original coastal palette outside the target envelope;
- identical prompt and source set for all three scheduled outputs;
- no crop and no target contact with a canvas edge.

Minor reconstruction noise outside the target may be observed and evaluated; it is not an authorized redesign.

## Working exclusions

- no sky, cloud, weather, or atmospheric alteration;
- no surfboard, sign, vehicle, railing, clothing, or secondary stamps;
- no additional emblem echoes, decorative lion fragments, or generic gold arches;
- no generalized black-and-gold wash;
- no typography or extra branding;
- no new objects, composition change, camera shift, or broad scene redesign;
- no descendant source, best-of source, crop-based repair, or manual concealment of a defect.

- Three independent outputs.
- Every output starts from the exact original base scene and exact canonical logo master.

## Advisory evaluation gates

### Critical gates

- correct source authorities and no descendant contamination;
- complete emblem containment with deliberate edge clearance;
- exactly one authorized motif;
- recognizable canonical topology from shape rather than color;
- preservation of the base scene and no unauthorized changes;
- no truncation, invented lion anatomy, fused parts, or generic substitute geometry.

### Integration gates

- coherent perspective on the selected surface;
- plausible architectural material, depth, light, and shadow;
- scene-native visual hierarchy without palette overreach;
- a result Nate considers a valuable Legacy transformation rather than merely a visible logo application.

Three of three conforming outcomes are the default evidentiary target, not an automatic advancement or release trigger. Nate may advance, revise, repeat, narrow the rollout scope, or stop after reviewing the complete distribution.

## Hard stop and repair boundary

- Hard stop after the three scheduled outputs.
- Wrong master, source blending, descendant contamination, or jurisdiction violation stops the cycle immediately.
- Do not rescue an output through cropping, cloud additions, prompt stacking, manual logo replacement, or promotion of the best descendant.
- Any corrective prompt change creates a new version. A revised core prompt must re-run a complete three-output calibration from the original base.

## Minimum stable-rollout pathway

The earliest defensible release is a **human-gated architectural Legacyify beta**, not an autonomous or universal capability.

### Stage 1 — Mechanism calibration: `L0-E`

- Three outputs on the controlled beach scene.
- Default advancement evidence: `3/3` clear the critical gates and Nate judges the distribution stable enough to test elsewhere.
- Failure does not authorize repair inside the cycle.

### Stage 2 — Holdout transfer

- Two materially different, previously unused unbranded scenes with suitable architectural surfaces.
- Two independent outputs per scene using the qualified core schema: four outputs total.
- Default advancement evidence: at least `3/4` are release-worthy, neither scene fails both outputs, and no critical defect repeats.
- Scene-specific variables are restricted to target envelope, scale, material, and placement.

### Stage 3 — Controlled real-request pilot

- Five actual Legacyify requests within the validated architectural scope.
- Maximum two attempts per request; every second attempt must restart from the original base, never the failed descendant.
- Default advancement evidence: every released image clears the critical gates and at least `4/5` requests reach Nate's approval within two attempts.
- Stability is judged as a reliable human-controlled workflow, not flawless first-generation behavior.

### Stage 4 — Human-gated `Legacyify v0.1` beta

- Release only the validated single architectural-embodiment mechanism.
- Keep Nate's approval gate on every output.
- Log the next ten real requests for truncation, topology drift, base-scene drift, motif multiplication, attempts-to-approval, and rejection cause.
- Pause or narrow the beta if a repeated critical defect appears.

Minimum evidence before the human-gated beta is 12 generated candidates: 3 calibration outputs, 4 holdout outputs, and 5 first attempts in the real-request pilot. The pilot may require up to 5 logged second attempts. This is an operational qualification, not statistical proof of universal reliability.

Perfect stamping, clouds, natural-material embodiment, characters, multi-source compositions, and universal scene coverage remain separate capabilities. They cannot delay the minimum architectural rollout unless Nate chooses to expand the release scope.

- every repair restarts from the original base;
- any core-prompt change triggers requalification;
- release scope and unsupported mechanisms are explicit.
