## What Legacy should look like

- Premium, intentional, visibly crafted commercial finish; confident, powerful presence; clear visual hierarchy and immediately readable silhouettes.
- Warm highlights against cool or deep structural values. Rich metallic gold or golden-orange is a restrained accent, never a replacement for composition or structure.
- Credible material and light behavior at the chosen medium's own level of detail. No universal PBR, cel outlines, volumetrics, pixel clusters, or painterly brushwork imposed across every modality.
- Office-derived abstract qualities are explicitly promoted across media: dark-luxury discipline, black/charcoal grounding where the scene permits it, warm gold/golden-orange accents, warm/cool light contrast, polished finish, restrained brand readability. Office furniture, room layout, equipment, props, photographic appearance, and embedded marks are **not** thereby promoted to unrelated scenes.
- Canonical emblem shape outranks palette. One complete deliberate embodiment outranks several approximate repetitions. Truncated marks are critical failures. Literal marks require exact geometry, typography, composition, spacing and negative spaces. Scene-native motifs may deform modestly for material/perspective but must retain bilateral relationship, silhouettes, central rise, and negative-space lineage.

## Three action contracts

### 1. Legacyify Character — controlled character performance

Input: one canonical lion/lioness identity master and one clear allowed pose/expression/wardrobe instruction; an optional supplied image may control pose/composition but never the identity. Profile: `ILLUSTRATED_CHARACTER`.

Best first demonstration: the male in one clear full-body or three-quarter standing action with a readable relaxed/welcoming gesture, followed by a distinct expression or restrained wardrobe change from the original master. Freeze each brief before its single request. Avoid simultaneous extreme pose, new clothing, strong emotion, new angle, complex props and occlusion.

Art: adult heroic commercial mascot, semi-realistic illustration, clean confident curves, readable silhouette, controlled outline where appropriate, soft cel shading/limited gradients describing fur and muscle. Use fitted black clothing/gold accents only in the authorized variable layer. No generic lion substitution, inflated anatomy, giant eyes, sketch noise, muddy texture, or forced 3D anatomy.

Consequential: identity (facial proportions, muzzle, eyes/brows, mane/hair shape, ears, tail, body proportions), species/character count, intended action and any message-bearing content. Perceptually important: framing, master rendering style, overall silhouette. Incidental: small fur microtexture or background texture when neither is an identity anchor.

Boundary to say plainly: strongest prospects are one clear character, familiar view, restrained change, visible face. Exact identity under extreme expression, major pose rotation, face occlusion, tiny subjects, or multiple interacting characters remains experimental. A nice generic lion is a failed identity result.

### 2. Legacyify Scene — preserve spatial story, change visual medium

Input: one legible source scene, selected output profile, and exact logo authority only when a motif is requested. Default source lock is subject/scene identity, composition/camera/frame, major buildings/terrain/roads/people/vehicles/foreground objects; redesign only explicitly released properties.

Recommend a stylized `PIXEL_ART` case for visible modality change, and/or an authored painted case under `PAINTERLY`; explicitly choose native pixel scale/cluster/detail frequency or brush scale/edge softness before the request. A scene with a few salient objects and a clear depth layout is best. The already-rendered office is a valid authorized low-risk case but a limited transfer test.

Pixel art: deliberate clusters, coherent edge stair steps, palette discipline and detail frequency; value grouping and controlled dithering; no PBR jargon, photographic texture, smooth vector emblem, subpixel lines or dense particles. Painterly: native brush/mass/light/atmosphere and consistent edge/detail scale; do not paste sharp graphic branding onto softer paint.

Consequential: focal subject, scene meaning, readable required text/branding, relationships that change the story. Perceptually important: crop, composition, camera, major geometry, salient object placement, recognizable light/atmosphere. Incidental: small non-message-bearing decorative texture, minor distant background detail, or micro-weather forms if predeclared and if drift does not change the intended experience.

Boundary: works most plausibly for legible simple scenes with explicit held properties and one selected medium. Dense crowds, intricate architecture, small exact text, physically exact reconstruction, and photographic preservation should not enter the initial supported claim. A black-and-gold wash is insufficient style conversion.

If adding an emblem, choose one scene-native surface with generous space and simple view. Preserve shape/topology and containment. Exact branding may be the hard limit of single-generation delivery: report it rather than silently fixing in an external editor or calling approximate topology exact.

### 3. Legacyify Together — distinct reference roles, coherent integration

Input: canonical female master for identity, office master or explicit alternate scene for environment, plus logo authority only if brand integration is required. Profile: `HYBRID_CGI_ILLUSTRATED_CHARACTER` (`CGI_ENVIRONMENT` + `ILLUSTRATED_CHARACTER`). This is the most ambitious action because multi-source validation had not started in the current dashboard.

Best first demonstration: one clearly visible lioness placed naturally into the office, grounded beside a desk or an open floor area, with a simple purposeful pose and a camera close to a canonical view. Preserve room topology and salient furnishings while releasing incidental texture and lighting details only as declared. Begin with one character, not the pair; a two-character scene can be a clearly labeled horizon demonstration afterward.

Integration contract: shared camera/perspective, scale, feet/contact, occlusion, light direction, cast shadow, local color interaction, edge hierarchy and finish. Preserve the illustrated character; do not make her 3D to match the room. Preserve authored CGI environment; do not flatten it into cel illustration to match her. Judge integration independently of the two component fidelities.

Boundary: one character with enough visible face/body and simple floor contact, in a scene with room to place them. Heavy occlusion, mirrored surfaces, multiple canonical characters touching, complex sitting/hand interactions, dramatic view invention and exact scene reconstruction remain experimental. Office content must not leak into unrelated supplied scenes merely because office abstract art direction is used.

## Mechanism and evaluation recommendation

Make the reusable action a short pre-generation compiler:

1. Resolve exact source files and assigned roles.
2. Inspect image; write a compact source-state ledger (focal subject; view/frame; major geometry; salient objects/people; light/weather; text/branding; relationships).
3. Classify consequential / perceptually important / incidental properties **before** generation.
4. Select a named profile for each component and state the redesign envelope.
5. Compile concise priority-ordered instructions: identity/brand/consequential → frame/spatial story → requested change → native medium → finish → exclusions.
6. Make one request, preserve untouched returned bytes, record request and output identifiers, then evaluate.

Do not feed a long evaluation report or dashboard into imagegen: historical R0-A2/A3 had wrong-output-class protocol boards. Request a standalone image explicitly and keep evaluation separate. Additional development requests get new attempt IDs and versioned briefs. A revision begins from original authorities, not a failed generated character as an identity source.

Show both judgments: **laboratory fidelity** (all meaningful departures) and **practical acceptance** (standalone coherent, intentional, finished, legible, no obvious AI defects, every consequential property correct). Changes already classified incidental can pass practical acceptance without disappearing from fidelity notes. Parent should use an independent visual evaluator for selected outputs and obtain no universal reliability rate from a changing small sample.
