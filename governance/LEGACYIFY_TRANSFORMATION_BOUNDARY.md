# Legacyify Transformation Boundary

## Purpose

This control governs transformation of an explicitly supplied, unbranded base scene into a Legacy scene. It extends the closed-source authority system without weakening any canonical-source lock.

The constitutional rule remains: **A source controls only its assigned component.**

## Authority layers

1. The supplied `BASE_SCENE_INPUT` controls the depicted subject, composition, camera, framing, and major objects unless Nate explicitly releases one of those properties.
2. `LEGACY_LOGO_MASTER` alone controls the Legacy emblem or logo identity introduced into the scene.
3. Any other canonical master applies only when explicitly invoked and only within its assigned jurisdiction.
4. Generated descendants remain evidence or production outputs. They do not become motif, identity, scene, or style authority unless Nate explicitly promotes them.


## Rendering-modality layer

- Production-facing successors use a deliberately rendered modality by default.
- A photographic input may control held scene properties without forcing a photographic-looking output.
- Literal photographic preservation requires an explicitly named `PHOTOGRAPHIC_PRESERVATION` validation profile.
- Every major component must name a profile from `governance/RENDERING_MODALITY_PROFILES.md`.
- Apply `governance/LEGACY_VISUAL_GRAMMAR.md` through the selected modality's native construction rules; do not impose CGI, cel-shading, painterly, or pixel-specific techniques universally.
- Input file type never selects the output modality.

## Two permitted forms

### Literal logo or stamp

A literal logo, badge, decal, engraving, print, sign, or stamp must be a precise embodiment of the authorized portion of `LEGACY_LOGO_MASTER`.

- Preserve the applicable canonical artwork, topology, geometry, proportions, spacing, negative spaces, and typography.
- Perspective projection onto a scene surface is permitted; improvised anatomy, fused or missing parts, generic substitutes, and hasty approximations are not.
- Keep the entire mark visible with deliberate edge clearance. Truncation is an automatic critical defect.
- Use only the number and locations explicitly authorized. Stamping does not authorize motif multiplication.

### Scene-native motif embodiment

The emblem may be reimagined through architecture, landscape, atmosphere, material, light, or another scene-native surface when that mechanism is explicitly authorized.

- The actual emblem shape is primary; the black-and-gold color scheme is secondary.
- Modest deformation is permitted only where perspective, material behavior, or environmental naturalness requires it.
- Preserve recognizable source topology: the mirrored/bilateral relationship, defining silhouettes, central rise, and major negative-space lineage.
- The result must read as native to the scene rather than as an unrelated generic flourish.
- Scene-native embodiment is not a license to reconstruct from memory or from a generated descendant; resolve the shape from `LEGACY_LOGO_MASTER`.

## Default base-scene lock

Unless a frozen packet says otherwise, Legacyify preserves:

- subject and scene identity;
- composition, camera position, and framing;
- major buildings, terrain, roads, vehicles, people, and foreground objects;
- the recognizability of the original environment.

Controlled redesign may affect materials, atmosphere, lighting, rendering modality, and secondary details only when its redesign envelope says so. An experiment may impose a tighter lock.

## Restraint and containment

- Introduce no logo location that was not requested or frozen into the applicable packet.
- Do not use a generalized black-and-gold wash as a substitute for the emblem's actual shape.
- Do not let one embodiment collide with, obscure, or force a crop of another scene element.
- Prefer one complete, deliberate embodiment over several approximate repetitions.
- Every generated result remains subject to separate Evaluation; no verdict belongs inside a Laboratory prompt.

## Pre-generation resolution

Before a Legacyify generation, identify:

1. the exact `BASE_SCENE_INPUT` bytes;
2. the exact `LEGACY_LOGO_MASTER` bytes;
3. the permitted embodiment form;
4. the authorized surface and motif count;
5. the selected rendering profile for every major component;
6. the held scene properties and permitted redesign envelope;
7. the explicit exclusions, integration rules, evaluation gates, and hard-stop rule.

If any required source or decision is unresolved, stop rather than substitute.
