# The Legacy — One-Pass Practical Reliability Direction

**Status:** ACTIVE PROGRAM DIRECTION  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-07  
**Authorized by:** Nate

## Purpose

Define the prospective V1 objective after closure of R0-A4. This direction governs future mechanism design and validation. It does not alter, reopen, or reclassify any completed experiment.

The constitutional rule remains:

> A source controls only its assigned component.

## V1 north star

The V1 objective is a Legacy-first transformation system in which an operator supplies one supported source image and makes one transformation request, and the system ordinarily returns one consumer-ready Legacy result without a retry or correction cycle.

The practical target is an output whose remaining differences are invisible or irrelevant to its intended viewers while its focal meaning, controlled identities, branding, and consequential scene facts remain correct.

V1 will establish the Legacy-specific system first. A brand-neutral framework may be extracted only after Legacy repeatability is demonstrated; generalization is not part of the initial reliability claim.

## One-pass definition

One pass means:

1. one operator action after supplying the source and required authorities;
2. internal source inspection, consequence analysis, and profile routing are permitted before generation;
3. exactly one accepted image-generation request;
4. exactly one returned output;
5. no hidden candidate batch, internal ranking across multiple generations, retry, correction, descendant edit, or external post-production dependency.

A source-authority, integrity, or unresolved-design hard stop may occur before generation. A stopped request is not counted as a successful one-pass output and must not be disguised as one.

GitHub, Drive, and Obsidian may support governance, evidence, continuity, and documentation. They are not required steps in the operator's per-image transformation path. V1 must not depend on migration to a separate native editing, rendering, or generation platform.

## Quality and reliability are separate

- **Practical quality** asks whether one output is consumer-ready under its intended viewing conditions.
- **Reliability** asks how often the unchanged mechanism reaches that threshold across eligible held-out inputs.

An output that appears approximately 85–90% complete in informal terms may qualify as practically perfect only when the remaining difference is inconsequential to the intended viewer and no mandatory protection is violated.

No arbitrary-image reliability percentage may be claimed from one excellent instance, a calibration set, or a changing mechanism. Any formal hit-rate claim requires a frozen mechanism, a defined supported-input envelope, held-out inputs, and a preregistered acceptance rule.

## Supported-input envelope

The long-term aspiration is that any still image can enter the system. Equal reliability across every possible input is not presumed.

Before validation, each mechanism must define the input classes for which it makes a reliability claim, including minimum source legibility and any known difficulty involving exact text, controlled logos, recognizable identities, dense crowds, occlusion, or missing visual information. Unsupported or high-risk inputs may still be explored but cannot silently enter the validated success rate.

The supported envelope should expand only through held-out evidence.

## Consequence-weighted preservation

Future packets invoking this direction must classify visible source properties before generation:

- **Consequential:** focal meaning, invoked canonical identities, branding, message-bearing text, safety- or decision-relevant facts, and scene relationships whose change would alter what the image communicates. These must remain correct.
- **Perceptually important:** composition, camera, major geometry, salient objects, atmosphere, and other properties materially supporting recognition or intended effect. These must remain substantially stable within the packet's stated tolerance.
- **Incidental:** details whose variation would not alter meaning, identity, brand integrity, recognizability, or the intended viewer's experience. Bounded drift may be accepted only when it creates no visible defect or downstream contradiction.

No canonical logo, controlled character identity, focal subject, or consequential fact becomes incidental merely because an ordinary viewer might overlook an error. When classification is unresolved, preserve the property or stop for Nate's decision.

## Internal one-pass orchestration

The intended user experience is one front door. Internal preparation may include:

1. source and authority resolution;
2. source-type and risk classification;
3. a source-state ledger covering focal subject, camera, major geometry, people, salient objects, weather, illumination, text, branding, and protected relationships;
4. consequence classification;
5. selection of the applicable Legacy transformation and modality profile;
6. compilation of a concise generation instruction with an explicit priority order;
7. one image-generation request.

This is adaptive routing behind one operator action, not authorization for multiple generation attempts.

## Evaluation tracks

Future one-pass work must keep two judgments distinct:

### Laboratory fidelity

Compare the output with its exact source and frozen contract. Record all meaningful retention, reconstruction, modality, authority, and compliance findings. This track preserves mechanistic knowledge.

### Practical acceptance

Judge whether the standalone output is coherent, intentional, visibly finished, immediately legible in its selected Legacy modality, suitable for its declared use, free of obvious AI defects, and correct on every consequential property.

Practical acceptance does not erase laboratory deviations. Laboratory deviation does not automatically defeat practical acceptance when the deviation is preregistered as incidental and is genuinely irrelevant to the intended viewer.

AI evaluation may support classification, but Nate's human judgment controls production suitability, advancement, and any change to the accepted variation envelope.

## Evidence and historical boundary

- G0-C1 remains an `EXCELLENT` controlled-instance result under its frozen rules; it does not establish one-pass arbitrary-image reliability.
- G1, R0-A3, and R0-A4 retain their accepted classifications and historical meaning.
- R0-A4 remains closed without correction or rerun. It is not a production-finishing candidate and no descendant may become authority.
- A future experiment may reuse an authorized upstream source under a newly frozen packet, but it may not reinterpret or continue an executed descendant.
- Practical-acceptance results and laboratory-fidelity results must both be preserved; neither may be substituted for the other.

## Current authorization and next decision

This record authorizes the program direction and the preparation of a bounded post-R0 mechanism decision surface. It does not freeze a successor experiment, approve a particular reference artifact, authorize generation, establish a supported-input envelope, or make a reliability claim.

The next decision surface should compare in-environment mechanisms capable of combining internal source-state analysis, consequence-weighted preservation, adaptive profile routing, and concise single-request instruction compilation. A content-neutral rendering-language control may be considered as one candidate mechanism, but it is not promoted or authorized by this record.

## Hard stops

- Do not convert the V1 objective into an external-editor or external-renderer workflow.
- Do not treat one operator action as permission for hidden multi-generation candidate selection.
- Do not lower identity, branding, or consequential-fact requirements to improve the apparent success rate.
- Do not retroactively reclassify historical experiments under practical acceptance.
- Do not claim reliability until an unchanged mechanism clears a preregistered held-out test.
- Do not freeze or run the next experiment until Nate selects the mechanism and authorizes its packet.
